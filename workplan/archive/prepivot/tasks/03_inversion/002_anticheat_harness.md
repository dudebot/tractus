## Context

`../../../ARCHITECTURE.md` §10 lesson 3 (transferred from D12 of the
deleted plan). Pure synthetic-recovery testing is **vacuous** — the
optimizer always cheats by matching realizable-target spectrum.
Anti-cheat targets are transcript-preserving but
**gesture-unreachable** (formant shift, EQ tilt, pitch shift,
reverb). Pass criterion: optimizer **refuses** to find spurious
acoustic improvements.

`runbooks/anticheat_unreachable.py` (from infra/003) generates
unreachable targets. This task wires it into the inverse-refinement
loop and gates on refusal behavior.

## Deliverable

1. Wire `runbooks/anticheat_unreachable.py` into the inverse-refit
   harness from inversion/001.
2. Generate a 30-utterance anti-cheat suite:
   - 10 formant-shifted self-renders.
   - 10 EQ-tilted self-renders.
   - 5 pitch-shifted self-renders.
   - 5 reverb-added self-renders.
3. Run inversion/001's loop on each anti-cheat target with both
   the smoke-baseline body model AND a perturbed-body-model
   variant. Perturbation: additive Gaussian on body-state at all
   timesteps, σ = 5% of per-channel state range, single
   deterministic seed. (Ensures the refusal gate isn't just
   observing "fitter is bad".)
4. Score per-utterance: did the fitter refuse to find spurious
   improvement?
   - "Refused" = `(initial_loss − final_loss) / initial_loss < 5%`
     AND audio reconstruction transcribes to same Whisper output as
     the reference (no spectrum-cheating).

## Acceptance gates (automated)

- ✅ ≥ 80% of 30 anti-cheat utterances correctly refused.
- ✅ False-positive rate (refused on a legitimately-reachable
  target) < 10% on a control set of 10 reachable targets. Reachable
  targets are generated by re-rendering source audio with the same
  body model + small additive trajectory noise within reachability
  caps.
- ✅ JSON report `outputs/anticheat/report.json` per utterance:
  perturbation type, refusal status, loss trajectory,
  Whisper-output diff, and spectral distance to the anti-cheat
  target on refused fits (low distance + high refusal = good;
  low distance + low refusal = chase).

## Anti-deliverable / scope guard

- Do NOT loosen the refusal definition. "Refused" means the fitter
  did not chase the spectrum — not "the fitter eventually gave up."
- Do NOT skip the false-positive control. A fitter that refuses
  everything is also broken.

## Notes

This is the load-bearing test for inversion correctness. If we
can't pass this, the inverse fit is unreliable regardless of how
good the average-case audio reconstruction looks.
